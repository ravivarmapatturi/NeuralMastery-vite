# Welcome to Neural Mastery

Neural Mastery is a single, holistic place to learn everything AI — from the
underlying mathematics up through cutting-edge agent architectures.

Start here: **[The Neural Mastery Roadmap](/docs/roadmaps/overview)** — the full learning path, in order.

## What you'll find here

- **[CS Fundamentals for AI Engineers](/docs/cs-fundamentals/overview)** — operating systems, concurrency, networking, distributed systems, Linux/Git internals, software engineering practice
- **[Python Engineering for AI](/docs/python-engineering/overview)** — OOP & functional Python, decorators & generators, async/concurrency, memory management, packaging, testing, tooling
- **[Mathematics for AI](/docs/mathematics-for-ai/overview)** — linear algebra, calculus, probability & statistics, optimization, algorithms & data structures
- **[Machine Learning](/docs/machine-learning/overview)** — classical algorithms, common problems and their state-of-the-art solutions
- **[Deep Learning](/docs/deep-learning/overview)** — architectures, training techniques, state-of-the-art models
- **[Computer Vision](/docs/computer-vision/overview)** — image fundamentals, the vision task taxonomy, modern/multimodal vision
- **[NLP](/docs/nlp/overview)** — classical NLP, word embeddings, the task taxonomy underneath modern LLMs
- **[Speech & Audio AI](/docs/speech-audio/overview)** — signal processing, spectrograms/MFCCs, ASR, TTS, speaker recognition, diarization
- **[LLMs & GenAI](/docs/llms-genai/overview)** — foundation models, training pipelines, prompt engineering, RAG, evaluation, multimodal models
- **[Agents](/docs/agents/overview)** — agentic patterns, MCP, A2A, tool use
- **[AI Evaluation](/docs/ai-evaluation/overview)** — benchmark design, golden datasets, LLM-as-judge, agent evaluation, human & adversarial evaluation, red teaming
- **[ML System Design](/docs/ml-system-design/overview)** — production architectures, scaling, real-world case studies
- **[MLOps](/docs/mlops/overview)** — data/experiment versioning, pipelines, containers, Kubernetes, CI/CD, monitoring, drift detection, LLMOps & RAGOps
- **[AI Security](/docs/ai-security/overview)** — the OWASP LLM Top 10, prompt injection, model extraction, data poisoning, adversarial examples, AI supply-chain attacks
- **[AI Safety & Alignment](/docs/ai-safety/overview)** — RLHF/Constitutional AI, reward hacking, specification gaming, deception, scalable oversight, frontier model safety
- **[Interpretability](/docs/interpretability/overview)** — SHAP/LIME, saliency and attention analysis, probing, sparse autoencoders, circuits, mechanistic interpretability
- **[Reinforcement Learning](/docs/reinforcement-learning/overview)** — MDPs, Bellman equations, Q-learning, policy gradients, PPO/SAC/TD3, offline RL, and how RLHF fits in
- **[Graph ML](/docs/graph-ml/overview)** — graph representation, node/link/graph-level tasks, GNNs, graph embeddings, knowledge graphs, Graph RAG
- **[AI for Science](/docs/ai-for-science/overview)** — drug discovery, protein structure prediction, genomics, materials science, PINNs and neural operators
- **[Domain AI Applications](/docs/domain-applications/overview)** — healthcare AI in depth, plus finance, semiconductor, cybersecurity, robotics, manufacturing
- **[Research Engineering](/docs/research-engineering/overview)** — how to read AI papers, and the paper timeline connecting this site's architectures historically
- **[Build From Scratch](/docs/build-from-scratch/overview)** — implement autodiff, attention, a GPT, a vector database, RAG, an agent, and more, yourself
- **[Projects](/docs/projects/overview)** — a six-level project ladder from a tiny afternoon build to a frontier capstone
- **[Databases](/docs/databases/overview)** — SQL (MySQL, Postgres), vector databases (ChromaDB, etc.), graph databases (Neo4j)
- **[Frameworks](/docs/frameworks/overview)** — PyTorch, LangChain, and other tools of the trade
- **[Interview Prep](/docs/interview-prep/overview)** — DSA, ML coding, knowledge Q&A, system design, behavioral
- **[Roadmaps](/docs/roadmaps/overview)** — structured learning paths tying every topic together
- **[Resources](/docs/resources/open-source-ai-resources)** — a running list of external free resources for extra practice

Pick a track from the sidebar to get started, or follow [the roadmap](/docs/roadmaps/overview) end to end.
